Select the numbers xi >= k from an increasing sequence x1 ... xn into c+1, c+2, ...
Source: A. I. Kitov, N. A. Krinitsky, Elektronnye tsifrovye mashiny i programmirovanie
        (1959), docs/ecm_i_prog.pdf, section 35 item 4 (forming operators), printed pp. 185-186.
Status: symbolic fragment following an operator Q1 that produced the sequence.
Φ6 forms the transfer command П7 (opcode 45) after the program has found
the first xi >= k. A * marks the readdressed field.

Data
  b)  k      b+1)  x1   b+2)  x2   ...   b+n)  xn
  e+1)  1(I)      e+2)  (n-1)(I)      e+3)  7777(I)
  e+4)  (b+n)(I)  e+5)  0000 0000 c+1 45
