Table of y = a0 sin^8 x + a1 sin^7 x + ... + a7 sin x + a8, Horner form in z = sin x
Source: A. I. Kitov, N. A. Krinitsky, Elektronnye tsifrovye mashiny i programmirovanie
        (1959), docs/ecm_i_prog.pdf, section 33 example 4, printed pp. 170-171.
Status: symbolic core. From this example on the book omits input, conversion,
        output and stop operators (footnote, p. 171).
Notation: one * marks addresses that depend on parameter i, two ** on parameter j.

  d = b + 10

The book keeps the name h+1 for the cell of O7(i) in the first program and
calls the last cell of F'9(j) g'+4.
