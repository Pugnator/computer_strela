sin x for |x| < π/2 by k terms of the Taylor series, with a repetition counter
Source: A. I. Kitov, N. A. Krinitsky, Elektronnye tsifrovye mashiny i programmirovanie
        (1959), docs/ecm_i_prog.pdf, section 34 example 2, printed pp. 175-176, table 38.
Status: symbolic core (input, conversion, output and stop omitted).
Method: u(i+1) = u(i) * (-x^2 / ((2i+2)(2i+3))), partial sums in c.

Data
  b+1)  x      b+2)  2      b+3)  1      b+4)  1      b+5)  k
b+3, b+4 and b+5 form the repetition counter; b+4 starts at 1 because the
first term u1 = x needs no computation. The result is left in c.
