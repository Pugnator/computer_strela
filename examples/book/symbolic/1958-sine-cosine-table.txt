Sine and cosine table for n = 1 ... 1000 (radians) from sin 1 and cos 1
Source: A. I. Kitov, N. A. Krinitsky, Elektronnye vychislitelnye mashiny (1958),
        docs/kitov_evm.pdf, printed pp. 52-55.
Status: symbolic. The book leaves the letters a, c, d, e unassigned
        ("Остается присвоить буквам конкретные значения").

Method: sin(n+1) = sin n cos 1 + cos n sin 1, cos(n+1) = cos n cos 1 - sin n sin 1,
checked by |1e-8| - |sin^2(n+1) + cos^2(n+1) - 1| >= 0 (formula 3.5).
A * marks an address that the program increases by 2 on each pass.

Data
  d-1)  1
  d)    1e-8
  d+1)  sin 1         d+2)  cos 1         d+3 ... d+3720)  results
  e+1)  0002 0000 0000 0 00    (2 in the first address)
  e+2)  0000 0000 0002 0 00    (2 in the third address)
  e+3)  d+3721 d+2 c+1 0 05    (final form of command a+1)
Working cells: c+1, c+2, ...

Program
  a+0)   d-1     0003    d-1     72     convert 4 data words to binary
  a+1)   d+1*    d+2     c+1     05
  a+2)   d+2*    d+1     c+2     05

  a+4)   d+2*    d+2     c+2     05
